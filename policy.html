Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and your choices.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoTravelling Travel Guide ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit our website. By using the site, you acknowledge this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: Your name, email address, and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Information collected automatically: Your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This information is commonly collected by websites and hosting providers.

How we use information

  • To reply to your messages.
  • To understand which guides are useful and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and help prevent spam.
  • To display advertisements and measure their performance.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners may use cookies to remember settings, measure traffic, support affiliate links, and display advertisements. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although some parts of the site may not work properly if you do.

Advertising

We may use Google AdSense and other advertising partners to display advertisements. Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings . You can also visit AboutAds.info to opt out of some third-party advertising cookies.

Analytics

We may use tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. These tools may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type, and referral source. We use this information in summary form to improve our content and website experience.

Affiliate links

Some links on DoTravelling Travel Guide may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. An affiliate company may place a cookie on your device to track a referral or sale. The affiliate company's privacy policy applies to information it collects.

Third-party links

Our site links to other websites for your convenience. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their content, security, or privacy practices. Please read the privacy policies of external websites before sharing information with them.

Children

DoTravelling Travel Guide is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the European Union, United Kingdom, or California, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, request corrections or deletion, or object to certain uses of your information. Contact us using the address below and we will review your request.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information in our care. However, no website, server, or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was most recently updated.
